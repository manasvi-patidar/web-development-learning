Black Holes

A black hole is a silent enigma, a region where gravity becomes so powerful that not even light can escape its grasp. It lurks in the vastness of space, invisible yet immensely influential, bending time and space around it like fabric caught in a whirlpool. Anything that ventures too close is pulled inward, stretched and swallowed beyond the event horizon. Despite their darkness, black holes illuminate our understanding of the universe, challenging the very laws of physics and inspiring awe at the mysteries that lie beyond human reach.
